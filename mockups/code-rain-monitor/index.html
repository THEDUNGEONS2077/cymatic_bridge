CODE RAIN EQ · C8 LAYERS · MONITOR VARIATIONS

You picked MONITOR. Six takes on it, all on C8 LAYERS. Each keeps the heartbeat (a spike with each kick, louder kicks taller, a resting heartbeat in quiet parts) and the glow along the horizon (following the music, brighter when loud). What changes is how the monitor sweeps and what its trace does. Pick one, or mix parts of them.

M1 · CLASSIC

The MONITOR you picked, for comparison.

  • A point sweeps left to right every 2 seconds, writing the heartbeat.

M2 · LOCKED

M2 · LOCKED

A hospital monitor: the trace stays bright until the point comes round again and wipes it.

  • Each sweep lasts two bars, locked to the beat, so the heartbeats stand still in the same places and get redrawn in step.
  • Eight beats across: calmer and more regular.
  • In the app it needs the song's tempo (a small addition to SIGNAL); until it locks on, it sweeps at a set pace.
M3 · CENTRE

M3 · CENTRE

Two points start at the centre, where the code streams out, and sweep out to both edges together.

  • The heartbeat is written in mirror image on both sides.
  • The focus stays at the vanishing point; the trace holds still while the points move.
M4 · SCAN

M4 · SCAN

The point carries a soft beam of light up and down the view, and the code it passes lights up.

  • On each beat the beam flares with the spike.
  • Ties the monitor to the code.
M5 · VITALS

M5 · VITALS

A patient monitor's three traces: the heartbeat on the horizon, a pulse wave above it, breathing below.

  • The pulse wave swells after each beat; the breathing follows the music's flow, deeper when it's loud.
  • One sweep writes all three.
  • The most like a real monitor; also the busiest.
M6 · RACE

M6 · RACE

The sweep runs with the music: faster when the bass and kick drive it (a racing heart), slower when they drop.

  • Spikes grow taller with the bass.
  • In the breakdown it slows to a resting pace.
OUTWARD and the first MONITOR: HEARTBEAT. Test track: 120 BPM, kick on every beat, snare on 2 and 4, off-beat hats, a bassline and a pad; bars 13–16 of every 16 drop the kick (a breakdown). Flashes are limited as the app limits them (at most 3 a second). Glyphs are mirrored katakana where the system has a Japanese font (MS Gothic on Windows), otherwise letters and digits. Mock-ups are drawn with Canvas 2D; the real scene will be WebGL on SIGNAL.
