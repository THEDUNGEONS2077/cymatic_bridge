CODE RAIN EQ · RUSH × WARP · DYNAMIC HORIZON

The three blends of C1 and C5, with a horizon instead of the light in the centre: a line of light across the view, through the point the code streams out of. HORIZON in the bar switches it three ways, in all three blends:

  • RIDGE: the horizon is the spectrum. It rises into peaks where the music is loud (bass in the middle, treble toward the edges), with a faint reflection below.
  • PULSE: a straight line of light, lit along its length by the spectrum, brighter as the music gets louder.
  • BANK: PULSE's line, and the whole view banks and drifts slowly, like a flight. In the app it could lean toward the louder stereo side.

In all three, each beat sends light running out along the horizon from the centre. The blends keep the rules: each stream listens to one band and its colour shows which; louder means faster and longer; heads flare on the beat. Pick a blend and a horizon, or say what to change.

C1's code, with C5's warp arriving on the beat: the streams lunge forward on every kick.

  • Without a beat it's C1: calm streams of code out of the horizon (watch the breakdown).

  • Surges overlap a little, so a busy track keeps faint streaks between beats.

C8 · LAYERS

C1's code in front, C5's warp behind it.

  • The streams of code stay as calm and readable as C1.
  • Behind them, thin lines of light fill the whole view and carry the speed: drifting specks when it's calm, long streaks when the beat surges.
  • Two depths of motion, one focus.
C9 · STRETCH

C9 · STRETCH

Code near the horizon, streaks near you: glyphs melt into light as they rush past.

  • Far off, the streams are crisp code closing in on the horizon.
  • As a stream nears you, its trail turns into a streak; the head stays a glyph.
  • Each beat surges: the streaks reach further in, then ease back.
  • The code spreads wider than C1's (C5's "everywhere"), with space kept round the centre.
