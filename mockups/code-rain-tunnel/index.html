CODE RAIN EQ · TUNNEL · SIX VARIATIONS

Six takes on C · TUNNEL. The rules stay the same in all six: each stream listens to one band, and its colour shows which (bass is the palette's first colour, treble its second); louder means faster and longer; heads flare on the beat; and a core at the centre is the one focus. What changes is the shape of the flight. Pick one, or mix parts of them.

PALETTE
BAR 1 · FULL
C1 · RUSH

C1 · RUSH

The TUNNEL you saw, for comparison: streams scattered round the core, rushing toward you.

  • Each stream is its own band, at its own place round the core.
  • A stream now fades out as it passes you, instead of vanishing.
  • The core glows with the level and pulses on every beat.
C2 · SPECTRUM

C2 · SPECTRUM

The streams in spectrum order round a ring: an EQ you fly through.

  • Bass at the bottom, treble at the top, mirrored left and right.
  • The kick thumps the bottom; the hats flicker across the top.
  • In the app, the left half hears the left channel and the right half the right.
  • The easiest to read; the most regular.
C3 · TUBE

C3 · TUBE

A tube whose walls are code. You fly down it, and it bends slowly.

  • Each column of the wall is a band; its wall glows with it.
  • Drops race along the columns toward you, faster when loud.
  • On every beat, a ring of light runs out of the core along the walls.
  • The far end wanders gently, like a wormhole.
C4 · VORTEX

C4 · VORTEX

The rush, twisted: streams curl in five spiral arms, and the whole tunnel turns.

  • Turning speed follows the level; each beat gives it a smooth push.
  • Trails sweep into arcs, so loud streams draw long curves.
  • The breakdown slows it to a lazy turn.
C5 · WARP

C5 · WARP

Code everywhere, not just round a ring, streaking past as you speed up.

  • Speed follows the level; each beat surges forward, then eases back.
  • At speed, trails stretch into streaks of light.
  • The breakdown drops to a slow drift through floating glyphs.
  • The biggest sense of speed; the busiest.
C6 · ECHO

C6 · ECHO

Rings of code, each one the spectrum at the moment it left the core.

  • A new ring every 80 ms; it bulges where its band is loud (bass at the bottom, treble on top).
  • The core is now; the last 2–3 seconds fly toward you as a tunnel.
  • On a beat, that moment's ring lights up white.
  • Built straight on SIGNAL's history ring.
Test track: 120 BPM, kick on every beat, snare on 2 and 4, off-beat hats, a bassline and a pad; bars 13–16 of every 16 drop the kick (a breakdown). Flashes are limited as the app limits them (at most 3 a second). Glyphs are mirrored katakana where the system has a Japanese font (MS Gothic on Windows), otherwise letters and digits. Mock-ups are drawn with Canvas 2D; the real scene will be WebGL on SIGNAL.
