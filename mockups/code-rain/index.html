CODE RAIN EQ · THREE DIRECTIONS

The second cymatic_bridge scene, three ways. Each follows the plan: columns of code rain, each listening to one band, colour showing which (bass = the palette's first colour, treble = its second); a column's loudness sets how fast and how long its drops fall, and heads flare on the beat. From PLEXUS: a calm stage, one focus, smooth. Pick one, or mix parts of them.

PALETTE
BAR 1 · FULL
A · CASCADE

A · CASCADE

The classic: rain across the whole screen, flat, every column its own band.

  • Loud bands fall faster with longer trails; quiet ones drift short and dim.
  • Heads glow near-white; on a beat the bass columns' heads flare.
  • Glyphs flicker more as the treble rises.
  • The closest to cyberdeck's MATRIX RAIN, made to hear.
B · STRATA

B · STRATA

Rain in three depths, as QUANTUM KERNEL layers its code.

  • Far: small, dense, dim and slow. Near: large, sparse, bright and fast.
  • Parallax gives depth; each column still listens to its own band.
  • Beat flares are strongest in the near layer.
  • Calm far back, the action up front.
C · TUNNEL

C · TUNNEL

PLEXUS's flight, as rain: streams of code rush out of a core at the centre toward you.

  • Each stream listens to its band: louder rushes faster with a longer tail.
  • Glyphs grow as they near you and fade past the edges.
  • The core glows with the level and pulses on every beat: the focus.
  • The most immersive in fullscreen.
Test track: 120 BPM, kick on every beat, snare on 2 and 4, off-beat hats, a bassline and a pad; bars 13–16 of every 16 drop the kick (a breakdown). Flashes are limited as the app limits them (at most 3 a second). Mock-ups are drawn with Canvas 2D; the real scene will be WebGL on SIGNAL.
