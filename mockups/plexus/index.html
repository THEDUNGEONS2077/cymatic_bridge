PLEXUS · THREE DIRECTIONS

The first cymatic_bridge scene, three ways. Each follows your answers: nodes scattered, each listening to one band, colour showing which (bass = the palette's first colour, treble = its second), bolder than NEURAL NET, and on each beat signals race along links, nodes swell and the whole net flashes. All three hear the same test track. Pick one, or mix parts of them.

PALETTE
BAR 1 · FULL
A · FIELD

A · FIELD

NEURAL NET's flat plexus, made bolder: a dense web across the whole screen.

  • ~1 node per 3,200 px², each linked to its 4 nearest and drifting on its own slow loop.
  • Nodes grow and links thicken with their bands; loud links glow brighter.
  • Each kick fires signals from the loudest bass nodes, 2–3 hops out.
  • Calm in breakdowns: the web stays, only the pad's mids glow.
B · DEPTH

B · DEPTH

The plexus as a cloud in space, turning slowly, near nodes big and bright, far ones lost in the dark.

  • The turn follows SIGNAL's flow clock: faster when the music is loud.
  • Depth fades links and nodes, so the cloud reads as 3D with no extra cost.
  • Signals race through the cloud, toward and away from you.
  • The beat flash lights the near side most.
C · CONSTELLATION

C · CONSTELLATION

Fewer, larger hubs in loose clusters, wired by the music itself.

  • A link lights only while both its ends' bands are loud, so the web rewires as the song moves.
  • Each hub wears a ring that opens with its band.
  • Signals are comets with fading tails.
  • Sparse and graphic: the easiest to read from across a room.
Test track: 120 BPM, kick on every beat, snare on 2 and 4, off-beat hats, a bassline and a pad; bars 13–16 of every 16 drop the kick (a breakdown). Flashes are limited as the app limits them (at most 3 a second). Mock-ups are drawn with Canvas 2D; the real scene will be WebGL on SIGNAL.
